LAUNCH RADAR

Pre-token protocols ranked by what their launch would be worth — every value term kept, every figure checkable against the snapshot.

How a row is valued — the whole chain, no hidden terms

Implied FDV = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × maturity factor × reach factor

Peer multiple. The market's own verdict: for the protocol's category, the median market-cap÷TVL of the protocols in this same snapshot whose token already trades. A category with fewer than 4 traded peers falls back to its family pool (vaults, credit, trading, staking), then to the whole market's median — and to a stated 0.25× constant if the snapshot held no traded protocol at all. Multiples are clamped to 0.05–2.50× before they value anything, and the detail view prints the raw median and the clamped value side by side, so the clamp is never a hidden term. The tier and the peer count are printed on every row; the peers themselves are listed in its detail view, labeled as the largest few of the full pool the median is computed over.
Growth factor. 1 + (7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%) × 0.4. Momentum is a term, not the ranking.
Maturity factor. 0.65 rising to 1.00 across 1,460 days of DefiLlama tracking: a protocol that has run for years with no token recorded is valued nearer its peers than one that appeared last month.
Reach factor. 1 + 0.03 per chain past the first, up to 7 extra chains.
Launch terms (priors, not snapshot readings). A float share by family (18–25% of FDV circulating at launch) gives the implied launch market cap; a community allocation by family (12–15% of supply) gives the implied airdrop pool. These two are the model's assumptions, distinct from the snapshot-derived terms above — so every detail view also shows the pool with both priors scaled a quarter down and a quarter up. Each plan prices itself in cash and hours, and the board's second lens divides the pool by that cash cost.
Grades and flags. The conviction grade (A–D) counts how the value was evidenced: its tier, its peer count, complete data, scale. Flags name facts of the record — declining TVL, tracked under 90 days, thin TVL, one chain, thin peers, missing data — never opinions. A tier's median is trusted from 4 peers up, but a row valued on 4–5 peers is still flagged THIN_PEERS: usable, and thin.
Who is not here. Products of another asset or protocol are excluded as non-launch candidates, by stated rule: a wrapped, staked or staking word in the name, an exchange's name first, a versioned release name, or a derivative category (bridges, liquid staking and restaking, exchange tokens). They belong to another protocol's launch, not their own.

A protocol here records no token in the snapshot (DefiLlama's record, fetched once and frozen); that is not proof it has none. An implied value is this board's own claim, worked out from the snapshot's figures — not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. Data: the sealed snapshot.

Board, detail and plan are written by one program from one snapshot: the page and board.json cannot disagree.
